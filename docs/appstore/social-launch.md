## 3. Video storyboards

### 3A. Social teaser: "The fold is the jug" (15 s, vertical 1080 × 1920, 30 fps, sound on)

**Shot:** the founder's real iPhone Duo, open, lying on a light wooden table, filmed from a **low three-quarter side angle** so the lid's lift and the stream crossing the fold are both visible. Soft window light. One hand only, fingertips on the top edge of the lid. Phone sound **on** (clip mic near the speaker). **Nothing physical on or between the screens.** Real hands are fine here (not an App Store asset).

| Time | Picture | On-screen text (big, bottom third) | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0–1.0 | Cold open: the Duo flat, jug full on the top half, glass on the bottom half, the dashed line glowing. The hand is already touching the lid | **Your phone is the jug.** | room tone, a soft glass knock |
| 1.0–4.0 | The hand lifts the lid slowly (~20°). The jug tips with it; the stream leaves the spout and **lands across the fold** | (held) | rush of liquid, glugs, the pitch rising |
| 4.0–6.0 | The level climbs toward the line; the lid starts lowering **before** the line; the tail of the stream lands | **Stop *before* the line.** | pitch near the top, the pour cutting off |
| 6.0–7.5 | It settles exactly on the line: sparkle ring, **PERFECT**, streak 1 | **Perfect.** | bright ting |
| 7.5–9.5 | The glass slides off; a soda glass slides in. A faster lift, foam climbs above the liquid and settles | **Soda foams.** | fizz, coaster clink |
| 9.5–12.0 | Hard cut: Rush, a flurry of three quick Perfects, multiplier ticks ×2 → ×3 | **One more go.** | three climbing streak notes |
| 12.0–15.0 | End card over the app's own menu: "Brim" wordmark, "Fold to Pour", "Out 17 Nov" (launch day: "Out now") | **Brim · out 17 Nov** | one soft ting |

Rules: no price in the video (it goes in the post text), no alcohol cues, no fast or repeated flapping. Keep the lid between flat and roughly upright. Export H.264, ≤ 60 MB (fits X, Bluesky, Mastodon, Threads, TikTok).

### 3B. App Preview: "Stop on the line" (optional for 1.0; 25 s, 886 × 1920, 30 fps)

Apple's rules: 15–30 s; captures of the app only (**no hands, no people, no device frame**); no prices; autoplays muted, so every beat has a caption on screen ≥ 1.5 s; poster frame chosen by us. The Duo's inner display (aspect 0.70) doesn't fill 886 × 1920 (0.46), so scale the inner screen to 886 px wide (about 1,260 px tall), centre it, and put captions in the cream bands above and below. Never crop into the game.

**Ships in 1.0 only if** it's recorded and checked by code freeze (Mon 9 Nov). Previews lock with the version, so a late one waits for 1.0.1. It needs a screen-recording step in CI (Squish has `preview.yml`; Brim doesn't yet: `DECISIONS.md` §3).

## 5. Don'ts

- No alcohol language or imagery, even as a joke.
- No real liquids near the phone in any video. No "try it with a real glass".
- No fast lid-flapping on camera, and no closing the phone shut as part of play.
- No "share to unlock", follow-for-codes giveaways or bought engagement.
- "iPhone Duo" only in referential use ("for iPhone Duo"), never as part of our product's name; no Apple logos.
- No price inside videos (keep it in the post text).
