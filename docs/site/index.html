How it plays

On iPhone Duo

Lay the Duo open on a table and lift the top half like a laptop lid. The jug drawn on it tips with it, and the stream crosses the fold into a glass on the flat half. Lower it to stop. The stream is still falling, so stop just before the line.

On every other iPhone

Drag up anywhere to tip the jug, and let go to set it upright. Same glasses, same physics, same leaderboards.

Pouring that feels true

  • The drink in the jug stays level, so a full jug pours with a small lift and a nearly empty one needs a big one.
  • The glass sings higher as it fills, so you can pour by ear.
  • Soda foams up and settles. Milkshakes keep coming after you stop. Potions need a steady hand.
  • Every glass gets a word: Perfect, Great, Good, Short, Over or Spill.

Three ways to play

  • Shifts across five venues, from the Lemonade Stand to the Potion Shop, and one more for those who collect enough stars.
  • Rush: one big jug, endless glasses, a Perfect streak that climbs to ×5.
  • Daily Pour: ten glasses, the same for everyone that day, one scored try. Share your row.

Kind to your hinge

Brim plays between flat and upright with slow, gentle lifts. It never asks you to close your phone, fold fast or fold over and over. Nothing real ever goes on the screen.

Pay once. That's it.

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts, no tracking and no data collected. Your progress stays on your iPhone. Every drink is non-alcoholic. Game Center is optional.
