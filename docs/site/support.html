Playing

How do I pour on iPhone Duo?

Open the Duo fully and lay it on a table, inner screen up, with the fold running left to right. The jug is on the far half and the glass on the near half. Lift the far half like a laptop lid: the jug tips and pours across the fold. Lower it to stop. The stream is still falling when you stop, so stop just before the line.

How do I pour on other iPhones?

Drag up anywhere on the screen to tip the jug, and let go to set it upright. Everything in the game, including every venue and achievement, works this way.

Is this OK for my hinge?

Yes. Brim plays between flat and upright with slow, gentle lifts, the range the Duo's hinge is designed to hold. It never asks you to close the phone, fold fast or fold over and over, and fast flapping only spills drinks. Never put anything real on the screen.

The game paused and says "Stand me up like a little laptop"

Your Duo is in book pose (the fold running top to bottom). Turn it so the fold runs left to right, lay it on the table and the game carries on.

Nothing pours

A phone lying flat never dribbles: lift the lid a little further. The emptier the jug, the further you need to lift, just like a real jug. On other iPhones, drag further up.

It keeps pouring after I stop

That's the stream still in the air, and thick drinks like milkshakes and honey keep flowing for a moment. Stop a little early and let it land.

How are glasses judged?

When the pour has landed, each glass gets a word: Perfect (right on the line), Great, Good, Short (well under) or Spill (over the brim). Perfects in a row build your streak.

How do I open the other venues?

Earn stars on shifts. Each venue opens once you have enough stars from the ones before it. There are no purchases, ever. There's also a secret venue for players who earn three stars on everything.

How does the Daily Pour work?

Ten glasses, the same for everyone that day. Your first run of the day is the one that counts for the score and the leaderboard; you can practise as much as you like afterwards. Tap Share to send your row of results.

Sound, feel and settings

No sound, or too much?

Brim plays sound even with the silent switch on, and mixes with your music. To make it follow the silent switch, turn on Respect silent mode in Settings. Sound and haptics each have their own switch in Settings.

Reduce Motion

Brim follows Settings > Accessibility > Motion > Reduce Motion: no screen shake and fewer particles.

Game Center

Optional. If you're signed in, your Rush and Daily Pour scores go to the leaderboards and achievements are reported as you earn them. Brim works fully without it.

Purchases and your progress

Will I have to pay again later?

No. One price, no in-app purchases and no ads. Updates are free.

Family Sharing?

Yes, Brim is available through Family Sharing.

Did I lose my progress?

Your progress lives only on your iPhone (and in your iPhone's own backups). Deleting the app deletes it, and we can't restore it, because we never had it.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
